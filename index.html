Idea to project,
from your pocket

Jolt sends the code you write straight to your ESP32, Pico W or any MicroPython Wi-Fi board. No laptop, no cable.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

Write

An editor made for thumbs

Python and HTML with one-tap shortcut keys, undo, and colouring that's readable on a phone.

Send

One tap to your board

Press play and your code runs on the board over Wi-Fi, with its output in a live terminal.

Start fast

Plugins with wiring guides

Sensors, switches and lights ready to run, each with the parts to buy and where the wires go.

Control

Build the remote too

Write a page with buttons, open it in Remotes, and control your device from anywhere on your Wi-Fi.

Fix

Open what's on the board

Browse the board's files, pull one into the editor, change it and send it back.

Keep

Projects that follow you

Jolt Pro saves your projects to your account so they're on any iPhone you sign in to.

How it works

  1. Set up the board once. A five-minute job on a computer puts MicroPython on it and joins it to your Wi-Fi. Jolt walks you through it.
  2. Find it from your phone. Jolt looks for the board on your Wi-Fi and connects.
  3. Write, press play, repeat. From then on it's all from your iPhone.
