Built for people who build

      

        
Write

Python and HTML with one-tap shortcut keys, undo, arrow keys and colouring that's readable on a phone.

SUPPORT

      
Questions

      

        
Which boards does it work with?
Any Wi-Fi board running MicroPython with WebREPL turned on: ESP32 boards, the Raspberry Pi Pico W and others. Jolt doesn't upload Arduino sketches, and it doesn't talk to boards running CircuitPython.

        
Do I need a computer?
Only once, to put MicroPython on a new board and join it to your Wi-Fi. Jolt's setup guide walks you through it. After that, everything happens on your iPhone.

        
What does Jolt Pro add?
Unlimited projects and remotes, every plugin, cloud save so your projects follow your account, and board alerts on your phone. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription through Apple, and you can cancel any time in Settings.

        
Something isn't working. Who do I ask?
Email epdesign08@gmail.com and a person will reply. Ideas for new plugins are welcome too.
